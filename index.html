The calculators and figures on this site are simplified estimates provided for educational purposes. They are not a pre-approval, a rate quote, an offer of credit, or financial advice. Payments shown cover principal and interest only and exclude property tax, heating, condo fees, and homeowner's insurance. Fixed-rate payments use Canadian semi-annual compounding. Mortgage insurance premiums are estimates based on published CMHC rate tables; provincial sales tax on the premium is payable in cash at closing and cannot be added to the mortgage. Rates shown are indicative, change frequently, and are subject to lender approval and your qualification. Ask me for today's numbers before making any decision.
